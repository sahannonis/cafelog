Coffee, fresh bakes and bingsoo

Cafe Log is a Korean coffee and dessert café in the Shops at New West, steps from New Westminster SkyTrain station.

We pile shaved milk ice high with fresh fruit, bake croffles and Basque cheesecake, and pour our signature banana and matcha lattes. Come for a quick drink between trains, or stay a while under the paper lanterns.
